Power BI Dashboard Embed

To embed your Power BI dashboard:

  1. Go to your Power BI workspace
  2. Open the report you want to embed
  3. Click on "File" > "Embed report" > "Website or portal"
  4. Copy the embed URL
  5. Replace the iframe src attribute with your embed URL
<iframe src="YOUR_POWER_BI_EMBED_URL"></iframe>
