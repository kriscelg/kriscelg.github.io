Agreement Monitoring Policy

Document Type: Policy
Last Updated: January 7, 2026
Department: Workforce Development & Training

Description

The Province delivers a wide range of programs and services. To do so requires the Province to enter into multiple agreements with municipalities, community-based organizations, and other third parties. These agreements commit public funds and create other obligations that must be monitored to ensure public accountability, protect the Province's interests, and ensure the agreement objectives are achieved.

Monitoring is a key part of the contract management cycle. It is an ongoing process that involves the systematic collection, verification, and analysis of information.

Guidelines

1. Purpose

This policy provides guidelines for monitoring agreements. The policy allows for a tailored risk-based approach to be taken for monitoring different types of agreements.

2. Application

This policy applies to all departments, agencies, boards, and commissions (ABCs) of the Province for all transfer payment agreements and procurement agreements for goods, services, and construction (collectively referred to as "agreements" in this policy).

3. Definitions

For purposes of this policy:

  • Agreement means a written contract or transfer payment agreement;
  • Agreement holder means the third party to an agreement including a vendor or a transfer payment recipient;
  • Contract management means the process of managing agreements from initial planning through to successful closure. This includes planning, negotiating, executing, monitoring, and closing or renewing;
  • Monitoring means the process of tracking and analyzing data relating to agreement performance to ensure compliance with agreement terms and conditions and to identify issues and make corrections as needed;
  • Risk means the possibility of an event or condition occurring that may prevent or restrict the delivery of an agreement and/or services, programs, or objectives of the Province. Risk relates to both probability and impact;
  • Transfer payment means a payment made to an individual or an organization as a grant, contribution, or other payment, excluding payments made in exchange for goods, services, or construction.

4. Agreement Monitoring Plan

An agreement monitoring plan shall be established for each agreement. The monitoring plan shall be commensurate with the risk and complexity of the agreement. The plan should address:

  • What will be monitored;
  • How it will be monitored;
  • When it will be monitored;
  • Who will be responsible for monitoring; and
  • What actions will be taken if issues are identified.

5. Risk Assessment

A risk assessment shall be completed to determine the level and type of monitoring required. The risk assessment should consider factors such as:

5.1 Agreement Value

Higher value agreements generally warrant more intensive monitoring. Consider both the total agreement value and annual payment amounts.

5.2 Agreement Complexity

More complex agreements, including those with multiple deliverables, milestones, or parties, may require enhanced monitoring.

5.3 Agreement Holder Capacity

Consider the agreement holder's experience, track record, and organizational capacity. New or unproven agreement holders may require more intensive monitoring.

5.4 Program Risk

Consider the importance of the agreement to program delivery and the potential impact if deliverables are not met.

5.5 Compliance Risk

Consider any legal, regulatory, or policy compliance requirements and the consequences of non-compliance.

Note: The risk assessment should be documented and reviewed periodically throughout the agreement term. Monitoring approaches may need to be adjusted based on changing circumstances or identified issues.

6. Monitoring Activities

Monitoring activities should be appropriate to the risk level and may include:

6.1 Review of Reports

Review of financial reports, progress reports, performance reports, and other documentation submitted by the agreement holder.

6.2 Site Visits

On-site visits to observe operations, meet with agreement holder staff, review records, and verify information.

6.3 Financial Verification

Verification of expenditures through review of invoices, receipts, and other financial documentation. May include audits for high-risk or high-value agreements.

6.4 Performance Measurement

Tracking of key performance indicators (KPIs) and comparison against targets or benchmarks.

6.5 Stakeholder Engagement

Engagement with clients, participants, or other stakeholders to assess satisfaction and outcomes.

6.6 Regular Communication

Regular meetings, calls, or correspondence with the agreement holder to discuss progress, challenges, and upcoming activities.

7. Documentation

All monitoring activities shall be documented. Documentation should include:

  • The monitoring plan;
  • Risk assessments;
  • Reports and other documentation received from the agreement holder;
  • Notes from site visits, meetings, and other interactions;
  • Analysis and findings;
  • Issues identified and corrective actions taken; and
  • Any amendments or changes to the agreement.

Documentation should be maintained in accordance with records management requirements.

8. Issue Resolution

When monitoring identifies issues or concerns, appropriate action shall be taken. This may include:

  • Discussing the issue with the agreement holder and working collaboratively to resolve it;
  • Providing additional guidance, support, or training;
  • Requiring a corrective action plan;
  • Adjusting monitoring frequency or intensity;
  • Withholding payments until issues are resolved;
  • Amending the agreement; or
  • In serious cases, terminating the agreement.

The response should be proportionate to the severity of the issue and aimed at protecting the Province's interests while supporting successful agreement delivery.

9. Roles and Responsibilities

9.1 Agreement Manager

The agreement manager is responsible for day-to-day monitoring of the agreement. This includes:

  • Developing and implementing the monitoring plan;
  • Conducting monitoring activities;
  • Maintaining monitoring documentation;
  • Identifying and addressing issues; and
  • Escalating significant issues to management.

9.2 Program Manager

Program managers are responsible for:

  • Ensuring monitoring policies and procedures are in place;
  • Providing guidance and support to agreement managers;
  • Reviewing monitoring results and issues;
  • Making decisions on significant issues; and
  • Reporting to senior management on agreement performance and risks.

9.3 Senior Management

Senior management is responsible for:

  • Ensuring adequate resources are available for monitoring;
  • Reviewing reports on agreement performance and significant issues;
  • Providing direction on policy implementation; and
  • Ensuring accountability for agreement outcomes.

10. Reporting

Regular reporting on agreement monitoring shall be provided to management. Reports should summarize:

  • Agreement performance against objectives and targets;
  • Financial status and expenditure tracking;
  • Issues identified and corrective actions taken;
  • Risks and emerging concerns; and
  • Overall assessment of agreement delivery.

The frequency and detail of reporting should be appropriate to the risk level and value of the agreement portfolio.

11. Continuous Improvement

Agreement monitoring processes should be reviewed periodically to identify opportunities for improvement. This may include:

  • Evaluating the effectiveness of monitoring approaches;
  • Incorporating lessons learned from completed agreements;
  • Updating tools, templates, and guidance;
  • Providing training and professional development; and
  • Benchmarking against best practices.
