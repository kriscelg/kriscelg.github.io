Training & Development

Access training videos, presentations, and learning resources to support your professional development

There are currently eight mandatory courses that all public servants are required to complete. You can access these courses through your Learning Management System (SuccessFactors) account. Please use your GOM email address to login.

Most mandatory courses only need to be completed once, within the first six weeks of employment. The two exceptions to this are Building Respectful Workplaces: Foundations and Anti-Racism: Understanding Ourselves and Our Systems, both of which must be completed annually.

Mandatory Course Completion Requirement
Welcome to Manitoba's Public Service One time, within the first six weeks of employment
Supporting an Ethical Environment in the Manitoba Government One time, within the first six weeks of employment
Information Security Awareness One time, within the first six weeks of employment
Building Respectful Workplaces: Foundations Annual, required by March 31
Anti-Racism: Understanding Ourselves and Our Systems Annual, required by December 31
Annual training options include:
  • New hires: Complete the eLearning course, Anti-Racism: Understanding Ourselves and Our Systems
  • Current Employees – options include:
    • Retake the eLearning course
    • Attend an anti-racism learning event hosted by the Public Service Commission – to be communicated annually in CONNECT or complete the recorded webinar available on the LMS following the event. Group training option by completing the intake form.
    • Attend an approved, department-coordinated session, ensuring the intake form is completed by employees or coordinators by December 31 following the training.
Our Shared Journey toward Truth and Reconciliation
  • Module 1: Understanding
  • Module 2: Engagement
 One time, within the first six weeks of employment
Additional modules may be assigned as required under management discretion. Please visit the Public Service Commission's Course Page for details.
Accessible Customer Service One time, within the first six weeks of employment
For more information on training please visit the Public Service Commission's Training Page.
