Our Mandates

The key mandates and responsibilities that guide our work

Employment Services

Provide comprehensive employment assistance services to help British Columbians find and maintain meaningful employment. This includes job search support, skills training, and career counseling.

Skills Development

Deliver skills development programs that enhance workforce capabilities and prepare individuals for in-demand occupations. We focus on bridging skill gaps and supporting continuous learning.

Employer Partnerships

Build and maintain strong partnerships with employers to understand labor market needs, facilitate job placements, and support workforce development initiatives that benefit both employers and job seekers.

Inclusive Programs

Ensure all programs are accessible and inclusive, with specialized support for persons with disabilities, youth, Indigenous peoples, and other underserved communities to promote equitable access to employment opportunities.

Performance & Accountability

Monitor and evaluate program effectiveness through data-driven insights and reporting. We are committed to transparency, continuous improvement, and achieving measurable outcomes for clients and stakeholders.

Innovation & Excellence

Foster a culture of innovation and excellence by adopting best practices, exploring new service delivery models, and leveraging technology to enhance program efficiency and client experiences.
