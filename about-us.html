About Workforce Development and Training

WDT helps Manitobans prepare for and transition to sustainable employment through workforce development programs and industry partnerships. The division works with employers, industry groups, educational institutions, training providers, and community organizations to strengthen Manitoba's economy by developing a skilled workforce and supporting business growth and productivity. The division offers targeted training, certification pathways, and employment services that respond to changing labour market needs.

TES

Training and Employment Services / Services d'emploi et de formation professionnelle

Designs and delivers a range of employment and training programs in partnership with community organizations, training providers, and industry to help Manitobans qualify for and connect with good jobs.

Service Areas

Winnipeg and Eastern Manitoba Service Area (WEMSA)

Includes Manitoba Jobs and Skills Development (MJSD) Centre Locations in the following areas: Steinbach, Selkirk, Gimli and Winnipeg.

Western and Northern Manitoba Service Area (WNMSA)

Includes Manitoba Jobs and Skills Development (MJSD) Centre Locations in the following areas: Flin Flon, The Pas, Thompson, Dauphin, Swan River, Morden, Portage la Prairie, Brandon and Westbran.

Provincial Services (PS)

Includes Employment and Training Partnerships (ETP) and Employability Assistance for People with Disabilities (EAPD).

AM

Apprenticeship Manitoba / Apprentissage Manitoba

Administers The Apprenticeship and Certification Act and coordinates the training and qualifications system that delivers accredited, structured, workplace-based skills and technical training to apprentices leading to journeyperson certification.

Units

Business Transformation

Provides advice, support and information on matters relating to identification of opportunities and modernizing business processes, strategic planning, innovation, and leveraging IT solutions including design, development, and deployment of technology within the branch.

Operations and Education Liaison

Responsible for apprentice and Trades Qualifier client case management, including: Registration review and approval processes and timelines; Review, monitoring and approval of Apprenticeship and Trades Qualification applications; Maintenance of client record files; Creating and maintaining relationships with apprentices and employers.

Policy, Legislation and Board Operations

Serves to develop policy recommendations to ensure that AM meets Manitoba's industry requirements for a skilled and productive workforce, and to develop new adaptations of the apprenticeship training model to improve accessibility.

Training Standards

Responsible for developing and maintaining the standards for all designated trades and occupations.

BITS

Business and Industry Training Supports / Aides à la formation dans les entreprises et l'industrie

Administers workforce development programs and supports to Manitoba businesses and industry.

Units

Business Supports Unit

Deliver programs to incentivize private sector investment in human resource development and workplace training.

Industry and Sector Supports

Provide industry grants that support companies to hire, train and retrain Manitoban workers to capitalize on growth opportunities.

ELMP

Economic and Labour Market Policy / Politique économique et marché du travail

Provides central policy, communication, leadership, and support on cross-divisional initiatives including contributing analytical expertise, maintaining inter-governmental relationships, and supporting strategic communications and stakeholder relations.

Units

Stakeholder Relations and Communications

Lead engagement and consultation activities with WDT stakeholders and the public.

Economic and Workforce Policy and Strategy

Provides policy support and coordination for economic, business and industry challenges and opportunities and administers programs that assist businesses expand, grow and innovate.

SIB

Service Innovation Branch / Services et innovation

Drives innovation and continuous improvement across all programs and services.

Units

Operational Supports Unit (OSU)

Delivers comprehensive support to frontline staff through programming, training, security and access coordination, reporting, and system enhancements.

Service Modernization Unit (SMU)

Leads modernization efforts throughout the division to enhance service delivery and operational efficiency.
